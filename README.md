# Hospital Stay & Discharge Delay Classification: An Operational Data Analysis Study

## Project Overview
This project analyses hospital length of stay (LOS) and discharge delays to identify operational bottlenecks and explore opportunities to improve discharge efficiency and patient flow.

Microsoft Excel was used for data cleaning, while Power BI was used for exploratory data analysis and visualization.

## Objectives
- Analyse hospital stay duration and discharge-delay patterns.
- Examine variables associated with discharge delays.
- Investigate potential bottlenecks in billing clearance and TPA-related processes where supported by the data.
- Develop visualizations to support hospital performance monitoring and operational decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel — Data cleaning and preparation
- Microsoft Power BI — Exploratory analysis and dashboard visualization

## Methodology
1. Cleaned and prepared the dataset using Excel.
2. Explored variables related to hospital stay and discharge processes.
3. Analysed discharge-delay patterns and potential operational bottlenecks.
4. Developed Power BI visualizations to present key patterns.
5. Interpreted findings to identify potential process-improvement opportunities.

## Business Relevance
Discharge delays can affect bed availability, patient flow and operational efficiency. Analysing discharge-related data can help hospital management identify areas for further investigation and improve process monitoring.

## Limitations
Findings depend on the available dataset. Observed patterns do not necessarily establish causation, and recommendations should be validated before implementation.
